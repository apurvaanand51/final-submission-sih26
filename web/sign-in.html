NETRA
Network-Enhanced Transaction Risk Analysis

An offline workstation for financial-intelligence and cybercrime teams.

Bitcoin traffic and chain data are fused on this host. Nothing is sent anywhere — not to a vendor, not to a model service, not to a font server.

Offline by design There is no outbound connection in this application, and CI fails the build if one is ever added.
Explainable Every priority is the exact sum of the factors behind it, and the parts are shown. An analyst can defend a lead; a reviewer can check it.
Audited Every view, decision and export is recorded with the capture hash and the model version that produced it. The log cannot be edited.
